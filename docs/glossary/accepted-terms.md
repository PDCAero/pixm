# Accepted community terms

Abbreviations accepted as names because they are generally understood across
aircraft performance and flight operations engineering (CLAUDE.md §5 principle 1).
Each needs CCB approval; the initial list and casing are an open decision
(`docs/decisions/open.md` #3).

| Term | Name casing | Full term and definition | Source | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| V1 | `v1` | Takeoff decision speed | 14 CFR 1.2 / CS-Definitions | Candidate |
| VR | `vR` | Rotation speed | 14 CFR 1.2 / CS-Definitions | Candidate |
| V2 | `v2` | Takeoff safety speed | 14 CFR 1.2 / CS-Definitions | Candidate |
| VREF | `vRef` | Reference landing speed | 14 CFR 1.2 / CS-Definitions | Candidate |
| VMCG | `vMCG` | Minimum control speed on the ground | 14 CFR 1.2 / CS-Definitions | Candidate |
| VMU | `vMU` | Minimum unstick speed | 14 CFR 1.2 / CS-Definitions | Candidate |
